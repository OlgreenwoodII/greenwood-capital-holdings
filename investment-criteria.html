Investment Criteria

What we look for.

A clear, disciplined framework that guides every acquisition Greenwood Capital Holdings makes, built around durable cash flow, operational quality, and long-term ownership potential.

Acquisition Profile

Quality businesses worth owning forever.

We acquire established, profitable companies with proven track records and clear paths to sustained growth. Our criteria are intentionally focused, we would rather own one extraordinary business than ten average ones.

01. FINANCIAL

Stable Cash Flow

Consistent, recurring revenue and sustainable EBITDA over multiple years, not one-time spikes. We prioritize businesses where the cash flow is durable through economic cycles.

02. REPUTATION

Strong Local Reputation

Long-tenured customer relationships, positive word-of-mouth, and a respected brand within the community the business serves.

03. OPERATIONS

Operational Upside

Clear, identifiable opportunities to professionalize systems, expand margins, or invest in growth, without disrupting what is already working.

04. MODEL

Service or Asset-Backed

Service-based businesses with sticky customer relationships, or asset-backed enterprises supported by real estate, equipment, or tangible holdings.

05. INDUSTRY

Target Industries

Agriculture, land services, laundromats, real estate-backed operations, essential local services, and other stable, recession-resistant industries.

06. TRANSITION

Owner Transition Situations

Founders or families exploring retirement, succession, or a partial liquidity event who want a respectful long-term home for the business they built.

07. GROWTH

Long-Term Growth Potential

A clear path to compound value through organic growth, geographic expansion, add-on acquisitions, or patient reinvestment of free cash flow.

08. TEAM

Strong Operating Team

A capable, motivated management team, or key employees ready to step up, that we can support, develop, and back over the long term.

Target Industries

Where we focus.

We concentrate on essential industries with tangible assets, durable demand, and proven business models, sectors where patient capital and operational discipline compound value over time.

Agriculture & Land

Land services, agricultural operations, ranching support businesses, and rural service providers tied to land-based industries.

Laundromats & Real Estate-Backed Services

Community laundromats, self-storage, car washes, and other recurring-revenue businesses operating on owned or controlled real estate.

Essential Local Services

Home services, light commercial services, facility maintenance, and other essential, non-discretionary local service businesses.

Real Estate-Backed Operations

Operating businesses with significant tangible asset coverage, small commercial real estate, light industrial, and owner-occupied facilities.

Specialty Service Businesses

Niche, recurring-revenue service companies with loyal customers, defensible market position, and clear opportunities for thoughtful expansion.

Add-On Opportunities

Smaller acquisitions that complement existing portfolio companies through scale, geography, customer base, or operational capability.

Process

Confidential. Direct. Respectful.

Every conversation with Greenwood Capital Holdings is confidential. We move at the pace that's right for the seller, never the other way around.

01

Introduction

A confidential conversation to understand the business, the seller's goals, and whether there's mutual fit. No commitment, no pressure.

02

Preliminary Review

Light, high-level financial and operational review under NDA, sufficient to determine if we can submit an indication of interest.

03

Indication & Diligence

Written offer with clear terms, followed by focused, respectful diligence. We work to minimize disruption to the business and team.

04

Close & Partner

Efficient closing followed by a thoughtful transition. We honor commitments, retain the team, and partner for the long term.
