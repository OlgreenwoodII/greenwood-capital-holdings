Careers

Build something lasting.

Greenwood Capital Holdings is a growing private holding company headquartered in Houston, Texas. We are actively building a small, capable team aligned by one shared conviction, that great businesses are worth owning for the long term.

Why Greenwood

A long-term home for long-term operators.

We are intentionally small, focused, and built to last. The work is meaningful, the equity is real, and the businesses you'd help us build will outlast all of us.

01

Permanent Capital

We are not a fund. There are no artificial timelines, forced exits, or quarterly performance theater, just the work of acquiring and operating great businesses over decades.

02

Real Ownership

Senior team members participate directly in the long-term value of the holding company. Compensation is structured to reward patient, disciplined contribution.

03

High-Trust Environment

Small team, high autonomy, low politics. We hire experienced operators and give them the space, capital, and support to do their best work.

04

Operator-Led Culture

We invest behind people, not spreadsheets. Every member of the Greenwood team is here to make our portfolio companies measurably better.

05

Houston Headquartered

Rooted in Houston, Texas, close to the industries we focus on and the communities our portfolio companies serve.

06

Generational Mission

We are building a multi-generational firm. The decisions we make today are intended to compound for the careers of those who help build it with us.
