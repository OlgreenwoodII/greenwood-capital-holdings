A team of experienced operators, capital allocators, and strategic advisors aligned by one shared conviction: that great businesses are worth owning for the long term.

Olvice Greenwood II

Founder & CEO

Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Greenwood Capital Holdings Inc., Olvice leads the firm's acquisition strategy, capital allocation, and portfolio oversight, with a focus on building a durable, multi-generational holding company headquartered in Houston, Texas. With a background in agriculture and agribusiness, he brings firsthand industry insight to the firm's investment approach, particularly in sectors tied to agriculture, land services, infrastructure, and essential operating businesses.

Our Approach

Operators first. Investors second.

The Greenwood team is built around operational expertise. We invest behind business fundamentals — people, processes, customers, and reputation — not financial engineering. That focus shapes who we hire, who we partner with, and how we engage with every portfolio company we own.

We are deliberately lean. Our model is to provide focused capital and senior-level partnership to outstanding operating teams — not to layer in unnecessary overhead. Every member of the Greenwood team is here to make our portfolio companies measurably better.
