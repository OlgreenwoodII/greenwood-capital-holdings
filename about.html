About the Firm

A holding company built for permanence.

Greenwood Capital Holdings Inc. acquires durable, cash-flowing businesses and operates them for the long term — with discipline, patience, and care for the people and communities they serve.

Overview

Who we are.

Greenwood Capital Holdings Inc. is a privately held investment and operating company headquartered in Houston, Texas. We acquire and operate a diversified portfolio of strong, cash-flowing businesses — building long-term value through patient ownership, operational excellence, and disciplined capital allocation.

Our long-term focus is constructing a balanced portfolio across service businesses, agriculture-related enterprises, real estate–backed operating companies, and other essential, stable industries. We believe the best returns come from owning quality businesses, supporting strong operators, and giving great companies the time and resources to compound.

We are intentionally not a fund. We are a holding company — capitalized to invest behind enduring businesses without the pressure of artificial timelines or forced exits.

M

Our Mission

To acquire and operate exceptional businesses with integrity, treat every stakeholder — employees, customers, sellers, and partners — with respect, and steward our holdings to compound value over decades, not quarters.

V

Our Vision

To build one of the South's most respected private holding companies — a diversified family of essential operating businesses that strengthen the local economies they serve and create lasting opportunity for the next generation.

The Holding Company Model

Patient capital, permanent ownership.

Traditional private equity is structured to buy, leverage, and sell businesses within a defined fund life — often five to seven years. That model can serve certain sellers well, but it is fundamentally misaligned with founders who want their company to continue, grow, and remain rooted in their community.

Greenwood Capital Holdings is different. Our capital is permanent. We do not have a fund to wind down or limited partners demanding distributions. We acquire great businesses and hold them indefinitely — making decisions on a generational timeline.

This structure allows us to invest in the long-term health of every portfolio company: people, infrastructure, customer relationships, and reputation — the things that quietly compound value over decades.

Long-Term Goal

Acquiring great companies. Growing them carefully.

We are building a diversified, multi-decade portfolio of operating businesses anchored in essential industries — companies that produce reliable cash flow, employ great people, and serve communities that matter to us.
