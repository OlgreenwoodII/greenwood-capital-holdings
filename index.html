What We Do

A holding company built for the long term.

Greenwood Capital Holdings Inc. is a private holding company that acquires and operates a diversified portfolio of cash-flowing businesses. We partner with founders, families, and operators who have built durable enterprises and are seeking a thoughtful, long-term home for their company.

Unlike traditional private equity, we do not operate on a fixed fund timeline. Our capital is patient, our horizon is permanent, and our priority is preserving the legacy of every business we own while creating room for it to grow.

Investment Focus

Where we deploy capital.

We focus on industries with durable demand, tangible assets, and proven cash flow, sectors where operational discipline and patient ownership compound value over time.

Service Businesses

Essential, recurring-revenue service companies serving residential, commercial, and municipal customers across the South.

Agriculture & Land

Land services, agricultural operations, and asset-backed rural businesses that generate stable, long-duration cash flow.

Real Estate-Backed

Operating businesses underpinned by real property, laundromats, storage, light industrial, and small commercial real estate.

Essential Operations

Time-tested local businesses with strong reputations, loyal customers, and clear opportunities for operational improvement.

Owner Transitions

Founder-led companies seeking a respectful succession partner that will honor the legacy and team they have built.

Add-On Acquisitions

Strategic tuck-ins that strengthen existing portfolio companies through scale, geography, or capability expansion.

The Greenwood Difference

Built differently. Held longer.

We are not a fund. We are operators and owners, accountable to the businesses we acquire and to the communities they serve.

  • 01
    Permanent Capital

    No fund deadlines. No forced exits. We hold businesses indefinitely, allowing decisions to be made on a generational timeline.

  • 02
    Operator-Led

    We invest behind operators, not spreadsheets. Our team is hands-on with portfolio leadership to drive measurable improvements.

  • 03
    Disciplined Acquisition

    We pay fair prices for great businesses. Every transaction is underwritten to conservative assumptions and strong fundamentals.

  • 04
    Legacy Preservation

    We honor what founders have built, the team, the name, the community presence, and grow it for the next generation.
